# Community Code of Conduct

The Nulx Linux community is expected to be professional, respectful, and focused on safe, reproducible technical collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other participants with respect regardless of experience or background.
- Keep reports factual, reproducible, and relevant to Nulx Linux.
- Respect privacy, authorization boundaries, coordinated disclosure, and applicable law.
- Accept technical correction constructively and distinguish verified evidence from assumptions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or publication of another person's private information.
- Sharing credentials, confidential data, malware, or unauthorized target data.
- Encouraging illegal access, destructive testing, or evasion of agreed rules of engagement.
- Spam, deliberate misinformation, or repeated disruption of issue triage.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the project or its community. Security-sensitive concerns should be reported privately through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
